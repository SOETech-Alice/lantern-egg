THE LANTERN KEEPER · BOOK ONE

Mo and the Lantern Egg

A parable about the crack that is not breaking

Watch the story light up, line by line — read along with it.

PAGE 1 · THE FINDING

Mo found it under the roots of the old pine: an egg made of stone, humming like a sleepy song.

It was warm. It glowed, just a little, at one thin crack.

Mo, Mo, small and slow. Wait with the egg. And watch it glow.

PAGE 2 · THE QUESTION

"Will you sing for me?" Mo whispered.

The egg only hummed. So Mo held it close, every day, and hummed back.

Mo, Mo, small and slow. Wait with the egg. And watch it glow.

PAGE 3 · THE WORRY

One night, the crack began to spread.

"Oh no," Mo said. "Did I break it?"

He held it tighter. The egg shivered.

Mo, Mo, small and slow. Wait with the egg. And watch it glow.

PAGE 4 · THE KEEPER

Kole the Keeper smiled. "Eggs are not squeezed open, little one. They are kept warm."

So Mo loosened his hands. He hummed HIS hum. Pip the firefly sat on the shell.

"The crack was not breaking," Kole said. "The crack was becoming."

Mo, Mo, small and slow. Wait with the egg. And watch it glow.

PAGE 5 · THE BECOMING

And on the night the valley was quiet, the egg opened — not with a crack, but with a song.

Light filled the whole nest. It knew Mo's name.

Mo laughed. "You were waiting for ME."

The little light glowed. Still, it glowed.
